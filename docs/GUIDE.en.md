## Open and collapse

Click the book in the library toolbar or the lower-right corner of a PDF to open the listening window directly. The bottom authors, headphones, papers, and gear icons open author networks, listening, references and the reading list, and settings. Hover over an icon for its name. All four main windows share these icons, with a theme-colored outline and icon for the current function. The active tab connects to its page; each page keeps its own controls above the navigation. Click outside the workspace in Zotero or the PDF, click the book again, or press Esc to collapse. Hover over the book for listening shortcuts and drag to reposition it.

Each document shows one expanded main panel at a time: listening, references, network, or settings. Switching panels transitions directly to the destination without stopping speech. Reopening references or the network resumes the existing view. PDF tabs and separate reader windows keep their own panel states. Detail and editing dialogs open above their parent and return to it when closed.

## Choose a reading scope

| Mode | How to use it |
| --- | --- |
| Selection | Select PDF text; release to start listening if automatic reading is enabled |
| Sentence | Select text within a sentence to hear the complete sentence; replay it as needed |
| Paragraph | Select within a paragraph to hear the full paragraph and set repeats |
| Full text | Read from the beginning, a selected position, or saved progress |

Hover over the book for quick controls. Hover over the leftmost mode icon to choose directly from a column of four icons; hover over an icon for its name. Pause and resume share one main button. Hover over it for sentence/paragraph navigation and Stop. Pause keeps your current position; Stop ends the current reading task. Adjust speed, repeats, and voice in the panel and voice settings. You can disable automatic selection reading in Settings.

The PDF must contain extractable text. OCR scanned pages first. Complex tables, equations, and unusual layouts may not read as expected. Text processing handles citation markers and running headers; figure-caption reading is configurable.

Language choices use flags; translation services and model providers use logos. Hover over a provider logo for its name. Select the LLM service and use the gear beside its logo to configure your provider and model. The LLM symbol changes to your provider logo after configuration. In Settings, the four category icons open reading and translation, voices, shortcuts, and appearance. Update options are at the bottom of Appearance.

## Compare a translation

Translation display is enabled by default. Hover over the language icon to choose a target directly. Click the current language icon once to toggle **Read translation**. Double-click to toggle translation display: a soft background marks the enabled state, and the icon remains available when disabled. Choosing a language without a bundled voice switches speech back to the original text while keeping translated text available. The translation target and interface language are separate choices. Targets without a bundled voice can still be displayed. Online translation requires connectivity; built-in services may rate-limit requests, or you can use your own model provider.

## Inspect in-text citations

Hover over supported numeric or author–year citations to inspect the referenced paper. Locate occurrences, open a local paper or DOI, or copy a formatted citation. Recognition depends on the PDF’s text structure. If it fails, open the reference panel or search by DOI/title.

Click or hover a paper title to expand its abstract. Abstracts support translation, source comparison, and listening. If an abstract is missing locally, query online metadata where offered. Paper Loom does not invent an abstract when a source has none.

## Read later and save

Add interesting papers to **Read later** and return to the list directly from your library, without opening a PDF. Open papers, inspect abstracts, copy citations, or save entries to Zotero. Check the title, authors, year, and DOI before saving: similar candidates are not necessarily the same paper.

Removing an entry from Read later does not delete the corresponding Zotero paper. Full-text access still depends on publisher permissions; Paper Loom does not bypass paywalls or sign-in requirements.

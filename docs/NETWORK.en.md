## Begin with your own library

Open the author icon in the bottom navigation and choose a library or collection. The graph uses authors and shared papers in Zotero items. Its first opening organizes the data; later visits reuse existing results.

<p align="center">
  <a href="images/network-en.png"><img src="images/network-en.png" alt="Author network: demonstration library" width="784"></a>
</p>

Search for a person or paper and select a node. Focus on an author to browse collaborators and shared papers. Pan, zoom, or use the overview control. Returning from details preserves the graph position.

The pictured authors and papers are demonstration data. Your graph reflects your collection, not every collaboration online. Check same-name authors against papers, affiliations, and ORCID.

## Continue into a public career

Open a career from author details or enter a valid ORCID. Browse available education, employment, and works, filter categories, inspect sources, and open papers. Closing the career returns to the network.

<p align="center">
  <a href="images/career-en.png"><img src="images/career-en.png" alt="Career interface with fictional demonstration data" width="784"></a>
</p>

Names, institutions, activities, and papers in this screenshot are fictional demonstration data. In normal use, information comes from records maintained by ORCID users; activities or dates may be missing. Absence of an entry does not establish absence of an activity. Responses are cached locally; refresh for current public records.

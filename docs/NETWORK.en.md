## Build a network from your library

Click the book → **Authors & careers**. The network uses authors and shared papers in your local Zotero items. A more complete library provides more connections. The first opening reads items and lays out the graph; subsequent openings can reuse existing results.

![Author network](images/network-en.png)

Search for an author or paper and select a node to inspect its connections. Focus on an author to explore collaborators and shared papers. Pan, zoom, or use the overview control to adjust the view. Open a paper, copy a citation, or refresh its details, then return to the same graph position.

Identical names do not always identify the same person. The graph reflects authorship in the current library, not a complete collaboration history or a basis for academic evaluation. Online enrichment is performed through the relevant online actions.

## Browse public careers

Use the career entry in author details or provide a valid ORCID. Browse available education, employment, and works on the timeline, filter categories, inspect sources, and open papers.

![Public career](images/career-en.png)

The screenshot shows Dawang Zhou’s public ORCID record (0000-0002-9053-5249). ORCID records are maintained by their owners and may omit activities or dates. Check names, affiliations, and sources. Neither a graph node nor a name-search result alone proves identity.

Careers open inside the network. Closing the timeline returns to the same author and view. Public responses are cached locally to reduce waiting; refresh online to retrieve newer content.
